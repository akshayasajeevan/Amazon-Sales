# Amazon Sales Analytics

### Interactive Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard

An end-to-end **Power BI sales analytics project** that analyzes Amazon sales data to identify trends, product performance, customer behavior, geographic performance, and key business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw Amazon sales data into an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed for business analysis and reporting.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance through multiple analytical perspectives, helping users understand revenue trends, product performance, customer contribution, geographic distribution, and order fulfillment.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Identify high-performing products, categories, and brands.
- Analyze customer revenue contribution.
- Understand geographic sales performance.
- Identify important sales trends and patterns.
- Present actionable business insights through interactive dashboards.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and analytical calculations
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data source and preparation

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home

A simple navigation page providing access to all analytical sections of the project.

### 2. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of sales performance, including:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Customers
- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Year
- Revenue by Category
- Orders by Payment Method
- Top Products by Revenue

### 3. Product Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes product and brand performance through:

- Revenue distribution by top brands
- Revenue contribution by brand
- Quantity sold by category
- Average selling price by category
- Product and category comparisons

### 4. Customer Analysis Dashboard

Provides customer-focused analysis including:

- Total customers
- Average order value
- Average quantity per order
- Orders by payment method
- Order status distribution
- Revenue distribution by category
- Top customers by revenue

### 5. Geographic Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes geographical sales performance using:

- Revenue by country
- Orders by country
- Revenue by city
- Revenue by state
- Geographic filters and interactive analysis

### 6. Insights Dashboard

Summarizes the major findings from the analysis and presents business recommendations covering:

- Product performance
- Customer retention
- Geographic opportunities
- Order fulfillment
- Product strategy

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis identified several notable patterns:

- **Total Revenue:** Approximately **91.83M**
- **Total Orders:** Approximately **100K**
- **Total Quantity Sold:** Approximately **300K**
- **Total Customers:** Approximately **43K**
- **Leading Category:** Electronics, with approximately **15.58M** in revenue
- **Highest Yearly Revenue:** 2023, at approximately **18.51M**
- **Top Customer Revenue:** Approximately **0.51M–0.55M** per customer
- **Delivered Orders:** Approximately **74.63%** of total orders
- **Geographic Analysis:** The United States contributes the largest share of orders and revenue in the dashboard

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Monitor high-performing product categories and brands to support continued sales growth.
- Develop customer retention strategies for high-value customers.
- Use geographic performance to support regional sales and inventory planning.
- Monitor order fulfillment performance and non-delivered orders.
- Review product-level performance to support pricing, promotion, and inventory decisions.
